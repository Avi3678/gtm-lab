Go-to-market

A voice AI's best salesperson is a phone call.

Avi Bedi / November 16, 2026 / Outside-in analysis

Nexor builds AI voice agents for sales and GTM teams, and the founding associate's sharpest tool is the product itself: let prospects experience the voice before they hear the pitch. The demo is a phone call, not a deck.

What I noticed

Nexor AI, an a16z speedrun company founded in 2026 and based in San Francisco, builds AI voice agents for sales and GTM workflows, and its founding team posted a GTM Associate Intern role in September 2026. Voice is a category where skepticism is rational: buyers have heard robotic call trees for years.

I'd test a call-first outbound motion: each target account gets a short, useful voice interaction built around their actual use case, a follow-up call that books the meeting or a qualification call that demonstrates the product, before any email. Measure meeting rate of call-first versus email-first. That is a channel hypothesis, not a claim that Nexor's current motion underuses its own product.

What I'd do in my first 30 days

Days 1-7. Learn the voice builder end to end by shipping a working agent for one real scenario, and document every rough edge a customer would hit.

Days 8-20. Run the call-first motion on twenty accounts, logging which scenarios produce callbacks and which feel gimmicky.

Days 21-30. Package the winning scenario into a repeatable kit and a teardown post the founders can point future prospects to.

I have run outbound campaigns for my own projects and built Prism AI, so I know the difference between a demo that impresses and one that converts. A summer 2027 internship doing call-first GTM at a voice AI startup is the sharpest version of that lesson.
