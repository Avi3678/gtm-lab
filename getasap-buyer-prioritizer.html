Fresh Buyer Prioritizer

Rank a sample outreach queue after applying practical route and order constraints.

Local, self-contained demo. No login, paid API, live AI or outbound messaging.

Fictional produce buyers and orders. No real route, customer, price or service availability is asserted. This is outreach prioritization, not vehicle-routing optimization or a quote.

Set the operating constraints

Eligible score = normalized order value × value weight + normalized nearby stops × density weight, scaled to 100. Constraints exclude before scoring.
