Dalus / Go-to-market

Write the brief for the engineer who inherits the mess.

Avi Bedi / November 16, 2026 / Outside-in analysis

My read: Dalus's outbound lands when the account brief speaks in requirements and traceability, not in SaaS benefits.

What I noticed

Dalus builds AI-powered systems-engineering software that lets hardware teams in aerospace, defense and robotics model and validate complex systems in one living digital model, instead of scattered documents. The founders lived the problem at Aerojet Rocketdyne and on a European Space Agency project, and Israel Aerospace Industries is a design partner. The GTM intern builds qualified lead lists, prepares account briefs ahead of sales conversations, and supports case studies and CRM hygiene. The posting is explicit that precision is the job: right company, right size, right title, verified contact.

I'd test a brief template that starts from a target's public engineering signals: a program win, a hiring spike in systems engineers, a certification milestone. Each signal implies a documentation pain, and the brief names it in one page, with verified contacts and no filler. Measure the quality of replies from engineering leaders against generic personalization. That is a process hypothesis, not a claim that Dalus's current research is shallow.

What I'd do in my first 30 days

Days 1-7. Learn the ideal customer profile, the design-partner story and the vocabulary of model-based systems engineering. Review existing briefs and CRM state before changing anything.

Days 8-20. Build one researched account list and three briefs in the signal-first template. Sit in on how the CEO uses them and tighten the format.

Days 21-30. Draft one case-study skeleton from an approved customer story. Report which signals predicted the best replies and make that the list-building rule.

Defending portfolio research to Congressional representatives in the SIFMA challenge taught me to write briefs that survive expert questions. I would bring that standard while learning aerospace sales from the founders.
