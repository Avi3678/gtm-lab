Sales

Every unmanaged device is a compliance story waiting to be told.

Avi Bedi / November 16, 2026 / Outside-in analysis

Swif.ai manages devices and governs AI agents for companies, and the sales intern's strongest open is the moment an IT lead realizes they cannot see what their own fleet is doing. Sell the audit before the platform.

What I noticed

Swif.ai (YC) helps companies manage every device and govern every AI agent, and it has hired sales interns in San Francisco for its summer program. Device and agent governance is a fear-driven category: buyers act when the risk becomes concrete, not when the feature list gets longer.

I'd test an audit-first sales motion: for each target company, a short assessment of their likely device and agent blind spots, drawn from public signals, hiring for IT roles, remote-work policies, app sprawl, and open with the one gap that hurts. The audit is the pitch. Measure meeting rate against feature-first outreach. That is a messaging hypothesis, not a claim that Swif's current sales motion lacks focus.

What I'd do in my first 30 days

Days 1-7. Learn the product against my own devices and write down what a first-time IT buyer would not understand.

Days 8-20. Build the audit-first outreach track for one vertical and run it with clean measurement.

Days 21-30. Report conversion by opening angle and package the winning motion for the team.

I placed 2nd nationally in the SIFMA competition, where risk only persuades when it is quantified, and I have run outbound campaigns for my own projects. A summer 2027 internship doing audit-first sales is exactly the training I want.
