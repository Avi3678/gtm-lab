Antela.ai / Go-to-market

In commercial real estate, the pipeline is the pitch.

Avi Bedi / November 16, 2026 / Outside-in analysis

My read: Antela sells AI agents into an industry that runs on relationships and spreadsheets, so its GTM should lead with a prospect's own pipeline math, not AI capability.

What I noticed

Antela.ai builds an AI operating system for commercial real estate, with products aimed at brokerages. Founder Amitesh Sinha, previously an engineering leader at Workday and Paylocity, posted in May 2026 that Antela was hiring interns in the San Francisco Bay Area to work directly with leadership on outbound outreach, lead generation, CRM and pipeline building, partnership research, customer engagement, and GTM initiatives.

I'd test a math-first outbound motion: for each target brokerage, build a one-page estimate of deals leaking out of their current workflow, slow follow-ups, stale listings data, manual comps, and open with that number instead of a product deck. CRE buyers respond to money left on the table. Measure reply rate against a capability-first control email. That is a messaging hypothesis, not a claim that Antela's current outreach is product-led.

What I'd do in my first 30 days

Days 1-7. Learn the product and the CRE buyer: shadow leadership calls, study the CRM as it exists, and write down the ten objections brokers actually raise.

Days 8-20. Build the pipeline-math one-pager template, run it on twenty target brokerages, and A/B it against the standard outreach on a small batch.

Days 21-30. Report reply and meeting rates by message type, document the research workflow, and load the next fifty researched targets into the CRM.

Researching an industry I did not grow up in and finding the number that makes a skeptic take a meeting is the part of GTM I like most. I would bring that discipline to Antela's outbound from week one.
