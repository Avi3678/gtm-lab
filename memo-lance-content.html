Lance / GTM & content marketing

Show the call the hotel would have missed.

Avi Bedi / November 16, 2026 / Outside-in analysis

My read: Lance's most convincing content is one real guest call resolved end to end, not another product walkthrough.

What I noticed

Lance builds autonomous AI agents that run hotel operations: a receptionist agent that answers calls, texts and emails around the clock, a sales agent that qualifies inbound inquiries and RFPs, and an operations hub for the property team. The company says it supports 50+ hotels across brands like Marriott, Hilton and Hyatt, and that hotels miss up to 40% of incoming calls during busy periods or after hours.

I'd test a recurring short-video format built around one missed-call scenario: the late checkout request at 11pm, the bilingual guest, the event RFP that arrived on a Friday night. Each piece shows what that call costs a lean front desk, then shows the agent resolving it, with the outcome a GM actually cares about: the saved booking, the recovered revenue, one fewer interruption. Claims stay tied to approved customer evidence. That is a content hypothesis, not an assertion that Lance's current content underperforms.

What I'd do in my first 30 days

Days 1-7. Learn the target property segment, the approved customer stories and the rules on claims. Agree on one scenario format and one metric that matters, such as qualified demo requests from operators. Review what has already been posted before adding anything.

Days 8-20. Produce two reviewed videos from real scenarios: script, record, edit, publish on the company page and the founder profile. Compare hooks and lengths, and keep a simple log of what each version earned.

Days 21-30. Test one distribution variable, such as timing around an industry event or posting in operator communities. Read the replies from hotel people, then expand the format only if it is starting real conversations.

At Flywheelr I built content workflows that generated 100K+ LinkedIn impressions, and I learned the post and the follow-up have to serve the same buyer. I would bring that discipline while learning hospitality operations from the team.
