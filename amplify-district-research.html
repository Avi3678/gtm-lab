One real public research example

What is known

  • Bridges and Amplify Desmos were selected for pilot, not adoption.
  • FAQ says Bridges August-November; Desmos December-March.
  • A Board recommendation is expected in Spring 2027. No adoption decision had been made in this FAQ.

These are planning windows, not a confirmed purchase date or an award.
