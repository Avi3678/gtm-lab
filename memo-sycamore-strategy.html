Trust is an enterprise agent's real feature. Operations should meter it.

Avi Bedi / November 16, 2026 / Outside-in analysis

Sycamore sells a trusted agent operating system to enterprises, and trust is not a slide; it is a measurable property of every deployment. The strategy intern's highest-leverage work is turning trust into numbers the sales team can stand behind.

What I noticed

Sycamore is building the trusted agent operating system for the enterprise, helping companies build, deploy, and orchestrate agents. It is hiring a Strategy & Operations Intern in the Bay Area on its GTM and Operations team, published in October 2026.

I'd test a trust-telemetry project: for each deployment, define the handful of numbers an enterprise buyer actually asks about, override rates, escalation accuracy, audit-log completeness, and build the internal view plus the customer-facing version. Procurement moves faster when the proof is already formatted. Measure sales-cycle friction points removed per quarter. That is an operations hypothesis, not a claim that Sycamore's current enterprise motion lacks evidence.

What I'd do in my first 30 days

Days 1-7. Sit in on sales and deployment conversations and log every trust question a buyer asks, verbatim.

Days 8-20. Prototype the trust telemetry view for one live deployment and validate it against the questions from week one.

Days 21-30. Ship the customer-facing version and a playbook for when the team shows it in the cycle.

I placed 2nd nationally in the SIFMA competition, where skeptical judges reward formatted evidence over confident narrative, and I built Prism AI. A summer 2027 internship where strategy means making trust measurable is the work I want to learn.
