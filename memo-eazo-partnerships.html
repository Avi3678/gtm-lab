Eazo AI / Creator partnerships

Creators do not want another tool; they want an audience.

Avi Bedi / November 16, 2026 / Outside-in analysis

My read: Eazo lets anyone build playable experiences with AI, so its partnerships motion should sell creators on distribution and play counts, not on creation features.

What I noticed

Eazo AI, founded by Yang Xie (previously at ByteDance) and based in San Francisco, turns ideas into playable, shareable interactive experiences, games, tools, and stories, with no code required. It posted a Creator Partnerships Intern role in June 2026, San Francisco hybrid and open to global remote candidates, pointing at a creator-led growth strategy where influential makers seed the platform with content.

I'd test a distribution-first pitch kit for creators: instead of leading with the builder, lead with what a creator's audience gets, a playable thing inside a post, and the retention numbers that follow. Recruit a first cohort of ten mid-size creators, co-publish their experiences, and measure plays and signups per creator. That is a partnerships hypothesis, not a claim that Eazo's current outreach leads with features.

What I'd do in my first 30 days

Days 1-7. Build several experiences on Eazo myself, study which existing content on the platform gets played and shared, and list the creator categories that fit.

Days 8-20. Draft the distribution-first pitch, reach out to a first cohort of creators, and get the first two co-published experiences live.

Days 21-30. Report plays, signups, and creator retention from the cohort, refine the pitch, and write the repeatable recruiting playbook for the next cohort.

I follow the creator economy closely enough to know creators guard their audience's trust above everything. A partnerships pitch that respects that, and leads with what their audience gains, is the one I would enjoy writing.
