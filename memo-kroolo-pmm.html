Kroolo / Product marketing

One workspace, ten replaced apps: show the before.

Avi Bedi / November 16, 2026 / Outside-in analysis

My read: Kroolo's claim is consolidation, and consolidation is best marketed as a concrete before-and-after: the tabs, tools, and costs a team actually drops.

What I noticed

Kroolo is an AI WorkOS that combines projects, search, and agents in one platform, positioning itself as replacing ten fragmented apps. Its founder posted in November that he is hiring three Product Marketing Interns to work in-house in San Francisco, a signal of real investment in how the product is explained, not just built.

I'd test a teardown content series: pick one persona per week, a startup ops lead, an agency PM, an engineering manager, and map their current stack tool by tool, with what it costs and where work falls between the cracks, then show the same week inside Kroolo. The persona's stack audit does the persuading. Measure demo requests per persona. That is a positioning hypothesis, not a claim that Kroolo's current messaging lacks specificity.

What I'd do in my first 30 days

Days 1-7. Learn the product deeply: run a real project in it, list the tools it touches, and study how the team currently describes the consolidation story.

Days 8-20. Build the first two persona teardowns, from public stack signals and team interviews, and adapt each into a landing section and a short social post.

Days 21-30. Compare engagement and demo clicks by persona, refine the audit template, and hand the team a repeatable teardown process.

I have presented research that had to survive expert skepticism, and a stack audit is the same discipline: every claim checkable by the reader. I would bring that rigor to Kroolo's product marketing.
