Venu AI / Growth & sales

Design for the conversation after the event.

Avi Bedi / November 16, 2026 / Outside-in analysis

My read: Venu's lead-generation proposition makes the post-event handoff a useful unit for growth work.

What I noticed

The homepage frames events as a way to meet qualified leads and describes using customer-persona pain points to attract the right audience. It also connects event interactions with follow-up sales conversations. That means a full room and a useful event are related, but not identical: the relevant question is which conversations deserve a next step.

I'd test a brief that works backward from one buyer problem and one qualified conversation. It would connect the invitation, session topic, interaction and follow-up rather than optimize registration in isolation. My hypothesis is that a consistent thread could make event value easier to demonstrate. The site does not tell me whether Venu currently has a follow-up or attendance problem.

What I'd do in my first 30 days

Days 1-7. Learn the priority event type and customer segment. Review how the team defines a qualified attendee and next step. Ask what attendee information can be collected and used with permission.

Days 8-20. Draft one event-to-conversation plan with a clear audience, useful topic and consent-aware follow-up. Map responsibilities and a simple handoff record; do not promise speakers, attendance or meetings.

Days 21-30. Support a team-approved test or analyze an approved past event. Separate registrations, attendance, relevant conversations and accepted next steps. Use the findings to revise the next brief.

At Flywheelr I worked on the path from content to enterprise conversations. I'd bring that follow-through and learn the practical work of event production from Venu's team.
