PhrasIQ / GTM, founder's office

Sell the audit trail, not the AI.

Avi Bedi / November 16, 2026 / Outside-in analysis

My read: in finance, the buying objection is never capability, it is accountability. PhrasIQ's GTM should lead with traceability, because that is what lets a CFO say yes.

What I noticed

PhrasIQ is building what it calls an Agentic Decision OS: infrastructure between enterprise data and human decisions, closing the loop from raw data to approved decision with a full audit trail. It started in finance, FP&A first, precisely because it is the most audit-sensitive domain, and is now expanding across the enterprise. The GTM & Sales Intern joins the founder's office, San Francisco or remote.

I'd test an accountability-first narrative in outbound: instead of leading with what the agents do, lead with what a reviewer can check, a variance explanation with sources, a forecast with its assumptions attached. Build one concrete before-and-after artifact per target persona and measure which artifact earns a second meeting. That is a messaging hypothesis, not a claim that PhrasIQ's current pitch is capability-first.

What I'd do in my first 30 days

Days 1-7. Learn the product, the FP&A buyer's language, and the founders' current pitch. Study two sales conversations end to end to find where deals stall.

Days 8-20. Draft the accountability-first one-pager for two personas, build a target list of mid-market finance teams, and run the first outbound sequences.

Days 21-30. Review reply and meeting rates by message version, refine the artifact, and hand the founder's office a documented sequence that can be rerun.

Presenting portfolio research to financial executives taught me that finance audiences buy what they can defend to their own reviewers. I would write PhrasIQ's outreach in that register.
