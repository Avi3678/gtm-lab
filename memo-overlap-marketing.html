Overlap / Marketing

Market with the product, in public.

Avi Bedi / November 16, 2026 / Outside-in analysis

My read: Overlap's best marketing asset is its own output. Every campaign should be made with Overlap itself, and the making-of should be part of the content.

What I noticed

Overlap builds AI video agents for media companies: it finds the best moments in long-form video and audio and turns them into clips that get posted automatically. The customer list is heavy: iHeartMedia, Barstool Sports, Yahoo, Almost Friday, The Volume. One public case study credits Overlap with 151 million new impressions for iHeart. The Marketing Internship, in person in San Francisco, hands the intern social channels, campaigns, events, and direct founder access.

I'd test a dogfood-in-public format: each week, take one long conversation, a podcast, a launch recording, or a founder interview, run it through Overlap, and post the results with a short note on what the agent picked and why. It demonstrates the product, fills the social calendar, and produces a repeatable before-and-after artifact for sales. That is a workflow hypothesis, not a claim that Overlap's current marketing underuses the product.

What I'd do in my first 30 days

Days 1-7. Learn the product hands-on, the customer case studies, and the current channel mix. Agree with the founders on the dogfood-in-public format and pick the first source material.

Days 8-20. Ship the first two cycles: clip selection, captions, posting across X, LinkedIn, and short-video channels. Log what the agent chose versus what performed.

Days 21-30. Turn the strongest cycle into a sales-ready one-pager and help produce one small event or activation. Report which clips drove profile visits and inbound.

I build and market my own projects, so the loop of making something and then making the content about it is familiar. Overlap would let me run that loop with a serious media customer list watching.
