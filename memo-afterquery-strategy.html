AfterQuery / Strategic projects

Frontier labs buy trust before they buy data.

Avi Bedi / November 16, 2026 / Outside-in analysis

My read: AfterQuery sells to research teams who benchmark everything, so its strategic projects should produce public proof of data quality that a skeptical researcher can check.

What I noticed

AfterQuery is an applied research lab curating data solutions for foundation model developers, and it is hiring a Strategic Projects Intern in San Francisco. The customer is a frontier lab or AI research team, a buyer whose job is evaluating claims for a living. Selling data quality to them is closer to publishing than to pitching.

I'd test a proof-artifact workstream: small, checkable demonstrations of dataset quality, a sample task with expert annotations, an inter-rater agreement summary, a contamination check, packaged so a research lead can evaluate it in ten minutes. Strategic projects here means turning internal rigor into external evidence. That is a proof hypothesis, not a claim that AfterQuery lacks rigor internally.

What I'd do in my first 30 days

Days 1-7. Learn the data pipeline, the quality metrics the team already tracks, and how current customers evaluated AfterQuery before buying.

Days 8-20. Draft the first two proof artifacts from approved, shareable data. Get research-team review and test them in one live sales conversation.

Days 21-30. Gather buyer feedback on what was convincing and what was missing, refine the artifacts, and propose the standing proof library.

Defending quantitative research to expert judges in national competitions is the closest thing I have done to convincing AI researchers. I would bring that evidence-first habit to AfterQuery's projects.
