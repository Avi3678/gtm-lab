Growth

Clinic staff know exactly what a missed call costs.

Avi Bedi / November 16, 2026 / Outside-in analysis

TriFetch's Linda answers phones and works faxes for specialty clinics, and the growth intern's sharpest asset is the number every office manager already knows: how many patients hung up this week. Lead with that pain, not the AI.

What I noticed

TriFetch is building an end-to-end automation layer for independent specialty clinics, covering patient calls and scheduling, and it is hiring a part-time Fall Growth Intern on-site in San Francisco. Independent clinics run lean: the front desk is one person doing three jobs, and every missed call is a patient who books elsewhere.

I'd test a missed-call-first growth motion: for each target specialty and neighborhood, open with the simple math of calls missed during lunch and after hours, then show Linda answering one. The demo is a phone call the clinic makes to itself. Measure booked demos per outreach. That is a messaging hypothesis, not a claim that TriFetch's current outreach leads with features.

What I'd do in my first 30 days

Days 1-7. Sit with the team on real clinic calls and write down the exact phrases office managers use for the problem.

Days 8-20. Build the missed-call-first outreach track for two specialties and run it with clean measurement.

Days 21-30. Report which specialty converts and package the motion, scripts, and numbers for the founders to keep running.

I ran my own e-commerce store to $5K+ in monthly revenue, and I learned that small operators buy back time, not software. A summer 2027 internship doing pain-first growth for clinics is exactly the craft I want to practice.
