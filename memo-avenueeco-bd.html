An all-in-one platform wins one workflow at a time.

Avi Bedi / November 16, 2026 / Outside-in analysis

Avenue Eco unifies HR, talent acquisition, workforce management, and revenue operations in one platform, and the BD intern's job is resisting the all-in-one pitch: pick the single workflow each prospect hates most and win that first.

What I noticed

Avenue Eco is building an intelligent platform unifying HR operations, talent acquisition, workforce management, and sales and revenue operations for modern teams, and it is hiring a Business Development and Growth Intern in San Francisco with hybrid flexibility. Platforms that do everything risk being chosen for nothing.

I'd test a wedge-first account motion: segment prospects by their most expensive broken workflow, slow hiring loops, messy scheduling, invisible pipelines, and open every conversation with that one pain, not the platform. Measure which wedge produces second conversations fastest. That is a positioning hypothesis, not a claim that Avenue Eco's current pitch leads with breadth.

What I'd do in my first 30 days

Days 1-7. Interview the team and any customers about which workflow actually triggered each closed deal.

Days 8-20. Build three wedge-specific outreach tracks and run them in parallel with clean tracking.

Days 21-30. Report which wedge converts and turn it into the repeatable motion, with talk tracks the team can keep.

I ran my own e-commerce store to $5K+ in monthly revenue and placed 2nd nationally in the SIFMA competition; both taught me that one sharp pain outsells ten nice features. A summer 2027 internship running wedge-first BD is exactly the craft I want to build.
