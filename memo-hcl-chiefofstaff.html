Chief of staff

A research lab's chief of staff protects the frontier from the calendar.

Avi Bedi / November 16, 2026 / Outside-in analysis

Human Computer Lab runs human-computer research and experimentation, and a chief of staff intern's real job is keeping the founders' attention on the frontier while everything operational still lands on time. Leverage is the metric.

What I noticed

Human Computer Lab is a private human-computer research and experimentation lab hiring a Chief of Staff Intern in San Francisco, full time. Research labs at this stage win or lose on focus: the experiments that matter compete with hiring, fundraising logistics, vendor wrangling, and a hundred small decisions a week.

I'd test an attention-audit operating rhythm: log where founder hours actually go for two weeks, then take over the three categories that most interrupt deep work and report weekly on reclaimed hours. A chief of staff who cannot show reclaimed attention is doing admin, not leverage. That is an operations hypothesis, not a claim that the lab's current time allocation is wrong.

What I'd do in my first 30 days

Days 1-7. Shadow the founders and build the attention log, plus a map of every recurring operational obligation.

Days 8-20. Absorb the first two categories end to end, vendors and scheduling first, and write the one-page playbook for each.

Days 21-30. Present the reclaimed-hours report and the next two categories to absorb, with the founders' priorities as the filter.

I ran my own e-commerce store to $5K+ in monthly revenue while carrying a full course load at Berkeley, so protecting deep-work hours from operational sprawl is personal, not theoretical. A summer 2027 internship as chief of staff at a research lab is the leverage education I am looking for.
