Paywall Comparison Lab

Show the difference, the uncertainty and the reason not to overclaim.

Local, self-contained demo. No login, paid API, live AI or outbound messaging.

Sample paywall test only, not Helium or customer data. Unadjusted two-arm normal-approximation interval, descriptive and unsuitable for repeated peeking, multiple variants or non-randomized comparisons.

Compare two paywalls

Turn the comparison into a test brief

Formula: p = subscriptions / exposures; absolute difference = pB - pA. Standard error = sqrt[pA(1-pA)/nA + pB(1-pB)/nB]. Displayed interval = difference ± 1.96 × SE, clipped to feasible -100 to +100 percentage points. No stopping rule, allocation check, refund data or revenue quality is supplied.
